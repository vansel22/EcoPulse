Built for a world with less waste.

A student-friendly prototype that combines data logging, personal targets, and decision-based learning.

🎯 The problem

Electricity, water and food can be wasted when we don't notice our daily habits. EcoPulse makes those habits visible and gives users a place to reflect and improve.

🧪 How it works

All records are entered by the user and saved locally using browser localStorage. The simulator uses illustrative scores rather than real-world engineering models.
